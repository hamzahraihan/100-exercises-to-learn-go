# Ticket Store

Data needs a home. Tickets move from variables into collections, and each
storage engine teaches its discipline: slices that grow and alias, maps
that index and demand allocation, cloning that severs sharing, sorting
that must not touch the input, windows cut against hostile bounds, and the
idioms that make registries work from their zero value. The in-memory
habits every later persistence layer assumes.
