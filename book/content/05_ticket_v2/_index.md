# Ticket v2

Failure as a value. Tickets grow a status, and every way Go reports
trouble gets exercised against it: sentinel errors with identities,
wrapping that preserves them, joining every complaint at once, and the
`Must` boundary where panics become answers. Short section, permanent
vocabulary — error handling stops being folklore here.
